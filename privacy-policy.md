## Scope

This notice covers the independent Zukki Test operated by Style International Corp. It does not describe separately operated team, Viwanja or third-party environments.

## Information and use

Depending on the features you use, Zukki Test processes customer WhatsApp identifiers, messages, shared locations, photos, videos and voice notes; conversations, requests, quotes and jobs; owner sign-in and business information; generated content; and connected Facebook or Instagram account identifiers and access credentials. Owner notifications also use device push tokens.

This information supports understanding requests, connecting customers with businesses, communication, job management and content preparation. Relevant request and conversation information is shared with participating businesses through these features. Social publishing requires owner approval.

## Services involved

The independent API is hosted on Render and uses a separate Supabase project for its configured database services. When enabled and used, the application's integrations use Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API for messages, Supabase for authentication and uploaded-file storage, and Meta model services for AI processing. AI features can send relevant conversation text and history, images, videos and voice audio to Meta for understanding, transcription and content generation. Hosting and service providers process information needed to deliver those services; this notice does not imply that every integration has been enabled or that every end-to-end feature is available.

When owner push notifications are used, Expo's Push Service receives device tokens and notification content, which can include request titles, summaries, approximate distances, identifiers and a thumbnail link. Map screens load resources from OpenFreeMap. Facebook and Instagram connections use Meta services for linking and publishing.

Deleting information from Zukki does not itself erase copies independently held by service providers, message recipients or people who downloaded content. Those copies are subject to the relevant provider's policies or recipient's handling.

## Retention and cleanup

You can request deletion using the instructions below. Removing an owner login does not by itself delete prior business messages, and uploaded files require a separate Storage deletion step.

Our current plan is to delete backups of the independent Zukki Test under our control 30 days after the contest ends. This is a planned cleanup, not a statement that deletion has already occurred or that it is automated. It does not establish a deadline for deletion of active records, provider-held logs or copies outside our control, and it does not mean that no backups exist.
