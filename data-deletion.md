## Request deletion

Email the address above. For customer-data requests, include the WhatsApp phone number used with Zukki, including its country code. Describe whether your request concerns customer data, an owner login, business data or a connected social account. We may verify your identity or authority as necessary before deletion. Do not send passwords, access tokens or verification codes.

Crystal receives requests for manual review and coordinates with the technical operator responsible for the relevant environment. The applicable records, authority and available deletion steps must be verified for that environment. This is an email-request process, not an automatic deletion button.

## Request scopes and technical review

The project technical operator has described the following deletion scopes. Their applicability and implementation must be checked for the independent Zukki Test environment before a request is carried out; this description is not a guarantee that every listed operation is currently available there.

- **Customer data:** deletion of the customer's number, chats, location, requests, jobs and quotes. Photos and videos require separate deletion from Storage.
- **Owner login:** deletion of the owner account and access. Prior messages remain unless the business is also deleted.
- **Business data:** deletion of business settings, jobs, quotes, messages, content and linked accounts.
- **Facebook or Instagram connection:** unlinking removes the linked-account record and its associated stored token. The technical operator reports that business deletion also removes these tokens.

Removing a login, deleting a job and unlinking a social account are not equivalent to deleting all associated data. State the scope you want in your request so these differences can be addressed.

## Limits and backup-cleanup plan

Deletion from Zukki does not recall downloaded files, content held by recipients or copies independently held by Meta or other service providers. It does not itself delete previously published posts from external platforms.

Our current plan is to delete backups of the independent Zukki Test under our control 30 days after the contest ends. This is a future cleanup plan, not confirmation of completed or automated deletion, and it does not guarantee removal of provider-held copies or logs. It is not a deadline for every individual deletion request.
